# Vanilla Templates Overview

This topic is the entry point for the `Templates / Vanilla` section.

It will document the block combinations that the app already supports for vanilla content, including:

- `HTML`
- `HTML-B`
- `HTML-FULL`
- `SVG`
- `Pug`
- `CSS`, `SCSS`, `SASS`
- `JavaScript`, `TypeScript`

Later phases will split these combinations into dedicated topics with focused theory and examples.

For now, this topic exists to establish the structure of the `Templates` chapter without mixing it with tests.
